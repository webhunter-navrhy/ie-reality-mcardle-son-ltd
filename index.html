Description

This beautiful 3-bedroom residence is exceptionally well located in the scenic countryside of Kilcurry. The property measures approximately c.212.5 square meters (c. 2,287.3 sq.ft.) and stands on a 3.8 Acres elevated site. The property has spacious and comfortable living accommodation together with a floored attic and mature gardens with a neighbouring tree-lined shelterbelt. Viewing is essential to appreciate this fine family home.

Accommodation:- The property contains, Porch, Entrance Hall, Reception / Sitting room, Family / TV Room, Kitchen / Dining, Garden Room, 3 Bedrooms, Bathroom, En-suite, Integral Garage, Utility Room and W.C (potential to convert garage into additional bedroom / granny flat or home office / gym). Additional features include a detached single garage, fully landscaped front & rear gardens with outdoor lighting and private water well.

This property is a very well maintained family home and presents a rare opportunity to acquire a substantial residence in this quiet rural setting. A short drive from national and secondary schools and perfectly located just 5 minutes from the M1 motorway (and 45 minutes to Dublin airport and less than 1 hour to Dublin or Belfast), and 10 minutes to Dundalk Train Station and Dundalk town center.
